# Project 1 - College Recruitment Improvement Study
Question: What are some ways to provide insights that can help the Information Technologies department increase enrollment in computing classes

To answer this question, I can use data analysis techniques to analyse data from 2024 detailing students studying/taking IT related courses and pursuing IT related majors as well as their demographics, how they heard about CCM, etc. The dataset contains data from a group of students, how they found out about CCM, their previous experience in coding classes, and more. I will analyse this data to answer the quetsions below

# 2 Data Science Questions

There are a few questions that I will be answering:
1. What are the order of courses that students enrolled in had the most participants in campus events or activities?
2. What is the order of majors that havethe most students who were exposed to computing classes in middle or high school?
3. What are the majors that have the fewest people who identify as women or non-binary?
4. From which source did the most students receive information about CCM’s computing programs?  Which source did the least students hear information about CCM's computing programs?
